- Source repository: https://github.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset

## Important media restriction

The source repository explicitly states that the image and GIF assets in images/ and videos/ are NOT covered by the MIT license. They are © Gym visual and require use under Gym visual's separate Terms & Conditions and licensing. The repository's NOTICE.md says:
- Media must remain at 180×180.
- Attribution must remain: © Gym visual — https://gymvisual.com/
- Cloning the repository does not grant RUBÍ Fitness Coach rights to reuse the media. Obtain the required license directly from Gym visual.

Therefore this project imports the exercise data/schema only. Do not copy, mirror, hotlink, or redistribute the Gym visual images/GIFs in RUBÍ Fitness Coach until the relevant rights have been obtained.
